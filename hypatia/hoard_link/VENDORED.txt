Vendored from HoardLink (https://github.com/Luissalet/HoardLink), version 0.3.0

Do not edit; replace the whole folder with a byte-identical copy of that
version to update.

Hypatia's Hoard talks to Hoard Link only through hypatia/backend.py, which
loads data/backend.json plus the environment into a LinkConfig for this app
and builds the cards_suggest prompt, without editing any vendored file. When
re-vendoring a later HoardLink version, drop in the new files here and
re-run `pytest tests/test_backend.py tests/test_suggest.py` to confirm the
wrapper still lines up with the public API (Link, LinkConfig,
CapabilityConfig, Resolution, Unavailable, BackendError).
